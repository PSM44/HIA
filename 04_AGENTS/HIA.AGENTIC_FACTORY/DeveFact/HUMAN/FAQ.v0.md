==========
FAQ.v0
==========
Q: Is DeveFact an app already?
A: No. It is currently a documented, script-assisted operating system for converting ideas into executable AI task packets.

Q: Does DeveFact call AI by itself?
A: No. Current local scripts do not call external AI.

Q: Can it push to GitHub?
A: No by default. Push requires explicit human decision.

Q: Can it modify a database?
A: No by default.

Q: What is a run?
A: A run is one controlled idea-to-task cycle stored under EXECUTION/RUNS/<RUN_ID>.

Q: What proves a step is safe?
A: A complete TOVS summary with SAFE_TO_PROCEED=YES and the correct EXECUTION_VERDICT.

Q: What should happen when blocked?
A: Stop the blocked task and create or review a Blocker Packet with options A/B/C.

Q: What is the next maturity step?
A: Harden the quickstart and then harden or export context packages.
