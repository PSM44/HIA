==========
HUMAN_DECISION_PACKET.v0
==========
STATUS: BATCH_0004_DRAFT

PURPOSE:
Give the human a compact, decision-ready packet when a blocker requires judgment.

STRUCTURE:
1. Decision required.
2. Why the decision is needed.
3. What is blocked.
4. Options A/B/C.
5. Expert recommendation.
6. Risk of doing nothing.
7. Expected result after choosing.
8. Exact command or instruction to continue.
9. Register entries that will be updated.

STYLE:
- Direct.
- No hidden assumptions.
- No exaggerated confidence.
- Include the expert recommendation.
- Make the default path obvious.

MINIMUM DECISION FORMAT:
DECISION_ID:
BLOCKER_ID:
SELECTED_OPTION:
RATIONALE:
APPROVED_BY:
DATE:
NEXT_ACTION:
